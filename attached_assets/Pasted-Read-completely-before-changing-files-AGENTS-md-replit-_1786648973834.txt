ACADEMIC YEAR MODEL

AcademicYear contains:

- unsigned auto-incrementing BIGINT primary key;
- foreign key to organizations;
- stable string `code`;
- required `name_en`;
- nullable `name_ar`;
- `starts_on` date;
- `ends_on` date;
- bounded string `status`;
- Laravel timestamps;
- no soft deletion;
- no database ENUM;
- no created_by/updated_by/deleted_by fields.

Approved statuses:

- draft
- open
- closed
- archived

Constraints:

- code is unique within its organization;
- start date must precede end date;
- stable code cannot be changed through ordinary updates;
- bilingual display names may change through controlled actions;
- archived records remain readable;
- inactive/deactivated organization cannot receive a new academic year through
  ordinary application behavior;
- no hard deletion.

SEMESTER MODEL

Semester contains:

- unsigned auto-incrementing BIGINT primary key;
- foreign key to academic_years;
- stable string `code`;
- required `name_en`;
- nullable `name_ar`;
- positive integer `sequence`;
- `starts_on` date;
- `ends_on` date;
- bounded string `status`;
- Laravel timestamps;
- no soft deletion;
- no database ENUM;
- no partial actor-audit fields.

Approved statuses:

- draft
- open
- closed
- archived

Constraints:

- semester code is unique within its academic year;
- sequence is unique within its academic year;
- sequence must be positive;
- semester dates must fall within the academic year;
- semester start must precede semester end;
- semesters in one academic year must not overlap;
- any positive number of semesters is allowed;
- never assume exactly two semesters;
- summer and exceptional semesters are permitted;
- stable code cannot change through ordinary updates;
- archived semesters remain readable;
- no hard deletion.

Do not enforce semantic codes such as S1/S2/SUMMER in the database. Those are
examples, not a fixed catalogue.

LIFECYCLE TRANSITIONS

AcademicYear:

- draft → open
- open → closed
- closed → open, requiring a non-empty reason
- closed → archived
- archived is terminal for ordinary actions

Semester:

- draft → open
- open → closed
- closed → open, requiring a non-empty reason
- closed → archived
- archived is terminal for ordinary actions

Additional lifecycle rules:

- only one academic year may be open per organization;
- an academic year must contain at least one valid semester before opening;
- opening an academic year does not automatically open its semesters;
- a semester may open only while its academic year is open;
- only one semester may be open within an academic year at one time;
- an academic year may close only when all its semesters are closed or
  archived;
- an academic year may archive only from closed;
- archiving an academic year must not automatically rewrite or delete child
  semesters;
- a semester may archive only from closed;
- an archived academic year prevents ordinary mutation of its semesters;
- opening, closing, reopening, and archiving occur through explicit actions;
- reopening reason must be passed through the lifecycle action for later Audit
  integration;
- do not create a partial audit system in F07;
- document that F18 will persist actor-aware audit history.

Do not silently cascade lifecycle transitions between parent and children.

DATE AND UPDATE RULES

- Dates may be changed while the record is draft.
- Ordinary date changes are prohibited after opening.
- Date corrections for opened/closed records require a future controlled
  correction workflow and are outside F07.
- Changing year dates must validate all existing semester dates.
- Changing semester dates must check parent containment and sibling overlap.
- Display names may be changed unless the record or parent is archived.
- Stable codes are immutable through ordinary application behavior.
- All multi-record checks and lifecycle changes use transactions.
- Use locking where appropriate without breaking SQLite tests.
